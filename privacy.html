Dusk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Dusk is a browser Web Extension that darkens web pages with CSS on your device.

  • We do not upload your browsing history.
  • Page darkening and site allow/deny preferences stay on your devices.
  • iOS may show a passwords/cards permission required for Web Extensions; that is Apple’s system prompt, not a request to send data to us.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs in the MVP.
